## Language Guardrails

- Say `stability-admissibility screen`, not final hydrate stability result.
- Say `occurrence classification` and `saturation regression` as planned or
  future linked ML outputs unless approved validation exists.
- Do not say hydrate proof, final saturation, validated sweet spots, or final
  ranking.
- Do not mention approved well-log/core rows, restricted identifiers, populated
  runtime paths, or trained models in weekday reports.
